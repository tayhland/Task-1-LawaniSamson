# Customer Orders Data Cleaning
# Objective
To inspect and clean a customer orders dataset by identifying missing values, duplicate records and formatting inconsistencies.
## Dataset
- Rows: 1,201
- Columns: 14
- Type: Customer Orders
## Cleaning process
- Checked for missing values
- Removed duplicate rows
- Standardised column names
- Verified data types
- Inconsistent formatting was confirmed
# Result
No duplicate records were found, column names were formatted properly, columns with missing values were replaced with values

# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview

This exploratory data analysis (EDA) examines customer purchase behavior, product performance, sales trends, payment preferences, referral channels, and order status using an e-commerce sales dataset. The objective is to uncover meaningful business insights that can support better decision-making through descriptive statistics and visual analysis.

---

# Univariate Analysis

## Distribution of Total Price

A histogram was used to examine the distribution of transaction values.

### Key Findings

* The distribution is positively (right) skewed.
* Most transactions fall within the lower to moderate price range.
* A relatively small number of high-value purchases create a long right tail.
* These expensive transactions significantly increase the overall spread of the data.

### Interpretation

The sales data indicates that customers generally make lower-priced purchases, while a few large transactions contribute disproportionately to total revenue.

---

## Box Plot of Total Price

A box plot was used to identify the spread of transaction values and detect potential outliers.

### Key Findings

* Several high-value outliers are present.
* The majority of transactions are concentrated within a relatively narrow price range.
* A small number of unusually expensive purchases lie above the upper whisker.

### Interpretation

The box plot confirms the positive skew observed in the histogram and shows that a few expensive transactions have a substantial impact on overall sales.

---

## Distribution of Quantity Purchased

A bar chart was used to analyze the number of products purchased per transaction.

### Key Findings

* Purchasing one item is the most common transaction.
* Four-item purchases occur less frequently.
* Five-item purchases are the least common.

### Interpretation

Customers generally purchase small quantities in a single transaction rather than buying in bulk.

---

# Bivariate Analysis

## Revenue Generated by Product

Product revenue was calculated by summing the total sales value for each product.

### Purpose

This analysis identifies which products contribute the highest revenue to the business.

### Insight

Comparing total revenue across products helps determine which products have the greatest financial impact and can support inventory planning and marketing decisions.

---

## Most Frequently Purchased Products

Product quantity was calculated by summing the total number of units sold for each product.

### Key Findings

* Chairs recorded the highest number of units sold.
* Chairs also generated the highest overall revenue.
* Desks ranked relatively high in purchase frequency but generated comparatively lower revenue.
* Phones produced the lowest total revenue.

### Interpretation

Revenue is influenced by both the number of units sold and the selling price. Products with high purchase frequency do not always generate the highest revenue if their unit prices are relatively low.

---

## Preferred Customer Acquisition Channel

A pie chart was used to examine customer acquisition sources.

### Key Findings

* Instagram contributed the largest share of customers.
* Other referral sources made smaller contributions.

### Interpretation

Instagram appears to be the most effective customer acquisition channel within this dataset.

---

## Preferred Payment Method

Transaction counts were grouped by payment method.

### Key Findings

* Online payment is the most frequently used payment method.
* Gift cards are the least preferred option.

### Interpretation

Customers show a strong preference for digital payment methods, suggesting that online payment remains the dominant choice during checkout.

---

# Correlation Analysis

A correlation heatmap was created using the numerical variables.

### Purpose

The heatmap measures the strength and direction of relationships between numerical features such as:

* Quantity
* Unit Price
* Items in Cart
* Total Price

### Interpretation

The correlation matrix provides a quick overview of how numerical variables relate to one another and helps identify features that may influence overall sales performance.

---

# Time Series Analysis

Monthly sales were calculated by resampling transaction data and a three-month moving average was added to smooth short-term fluctuations.

### Key Findings

* Monthly sales fluctuate considerably over time.
* Several noticeable peaks and declines occur throughout the observation period.
* The moving average highlights the underlying sales trend.

### Interpretation

Sales declined gradually from mid-2023 into early 2024, experienced a temporary recovery during mid-2024, remained relatively stable afterward, and showed signs of improvement toward the end of the analysis period.

---

# Order Status Analysis

A horizontal bar chart was used to visualize the frequency of each order status.

### Purpose

This analysis provides an overview of order fulfillment performance by comparing the number of:

* Delivered orders
* Shipped orders
* Pending orders
* Returned orders
* Cancelled orders

### Interpretation

Understanding the distribution of order statuses helps evaluate operational efficiency and identify potential areas requiring improvement within the order fulfillment process.

---

# Coupon Performance Analysis

Total sales were grouped by coupon code and visualized using a pie chart.

### Purpose

This analysis measures the contribution of each coupon campaign to total sales.

### Interpretation

Comparing sales across coupon codes provides insight into promotional effectiveness and customer adoption of discount campaigns.

---

# Overall Summary

The exploratory analysis reveals several important business insights:

* Transaction values are right-skewed, with a small number of high-value purchases contributing significantly to total revenue.
* Customers typically purchase only one product per transaction.
* Chairs are both the highest-selling and highest-revenue product.
* Instagram is the leading customer acquisition channel.
* Online payment is the preferred payment method.
* Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the period but display broader underlying trends when smoothed with a moving average.
* Order status analysis provides visibility into fulfillment performance.
* Coupon analysis helps evaluate the effectiveness of promotional campaigns.

These findings provide a strong foundation for further statistical analysis, predictive modeling, and business decision-making.
